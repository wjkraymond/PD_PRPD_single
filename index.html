The Jet Colormap Scale (Count n)

In a PRPD plot, hundreds or thousands of power cycles are superimposed. Because multiple discharges frequently land at almost the exact same phase angle and charge magnitude, we divide the canvas into discrete 2D bins:

Blue: 1 event (Infrequent / single breakdown)
Cyan / Green: 2–4 events (Growing recurrence)
Yellow: 5–7 events (Frequent discharge)
Red / Dark Red: 8+ events (Maximum repetition rate)

In the 3D view, this repetition count n is projected vertically on the Z-axis, creating a topographical mountain of partial discharge activity.

Cavity Void Defect Physics

Trapped gas cavities within epoxy or cross-linked polyethylene (XLPE) discharge when the electric field inside the void exceeds Paschen breakdown. Because the capacitive void voltage is in phase with the slope ($\frac{du}{dt}$), discharges cluster symmetrically in the 1st (30°–90°) and 3rd (210°–270°) quadrants.

Understanding PRPD (φ - q - n)

Partial Discharge (PD) refers to localized dielectric breakdowns that only partially bridge the insulation between conductors.

• φ (Phase Angle): Instant position in the 0°–360° power cycle.
• q (Apparent Charge): Size of the discharge impulse in pC.
• n (Pulse Count): How many pulses occurred at that exact (φ, q) bin.

By watching the animation slowly, students observe how stochastic micro-pulses in time-domain transform cycle-by-cycle into distinct geometric fingerprint shapes.
